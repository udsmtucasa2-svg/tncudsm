HOME HERO SLIDER PICTURES
=========================
Put your photos in THIS folder (public/images/hero/).
Then list them in:  src/data/heroSlides.js   (src: '/images/hero/your-file.jpg')

- Size: 1920x1080 (landscape) JPG, under about 400 KB each.
- Keep the main subject in the centre / right: the headline sits on the left.
- Use 3 to 6 slides. The four hero-1..4.jpg files here are brand-colour placeholders:
  replace them with real photos using the SAME file names, or edit heroSlides.js.
